PHYSICS 3D PRACTICAL

Simple Harmonic Motion & Damping

Why & How it happens: When a system is displaced from equilibrium, a restoring force proportional to displacement ($F = -kx$) pulls it back. In real-world environments, damping forces (proportional to velocity) gradually dissipate energy into heat, causing amplitude decay.

CHEMISTRY 3D PRACTICAL

VSEPR Molecular Geometry

Why & How it happens: Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion theory dictates that electron pairs surrounding a central metallic or non-metallic atom position themselves as far apart as possible to minimize electrostatic repulsion, giving rise to unique spatial configurations like tetrahedral or octahedral shapes.

BIOLOGY 3D PRACTICAL

DNA Replication & Enzymatic Action

Why & How it happens: DNA Helicase breaks hydrogen bonds holding nucleotide base pairs together (Adenine-Thymine, Guanine-Cytosine). DNA Polymerase then reads the template strand to synthesize leading and lagging strands, ensuring genetic fidelity.

Advanced Solved Research Examples

Step-by-step mathematical problem solving with real-world applications across disciplines.

1. Relativistic Kinetic Energy (Physics)

Problem: Calculate the relativistic kinetic energy of an electron moving at $0.8c$ (where $c = 3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$, rest mass $m_0 = 9.11 \times 10^{-31} \text{ kg}$).

Formula: $K = (\gamma - 1) m_0 c^2$ where $\gamma = 1 / \sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}$

Detailed Solution:
1. Lorentz Factor ($\gamma$): $1 / \sqrt{1 - (0.8)^2} = 1 / \sqrt{0.36} = 1 / 0.6 = 1.667$
2. Rest Energy ($m_0 c^2$): $8.2 \times 10^{-14} \text{ Joules}$
Result: $K = (1.667 - 1) \times 8.2 \times 10^{-14} = 5.47 \times 10^{-14} \text{ Joules}$.

2. Rate of Chemical Reaction (Chemistry)

Problem: For the reaction $2N_2O_5 \rightarrow 4NO_2 + O_2$, if the disappearance rate of $N_2O_5$ is $4.2 \times 10^{-4} \text{ M/s}$, find the rate of formation of $NO_2$.

Formula: $\text{Rate} = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{\Delta[N_2O_5]}{\Delta t} = +\frac{1}{4}\frac{\Delta[NO_2]}{\Delta t}$

Detailed Solution:
$\frac{\Delta[NO_2]}{\Delta t} = 2 \times (-\frac{\Delta[N_2O_5]}{\Delta t})$
$= 2 \times 4.2 \times 10^{-4} = 8.4 \times 10^{-4} \text{ M/s}$.
